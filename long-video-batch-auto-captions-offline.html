Auto captions for long videos and many files – offline

Online caption tools usually count minutes: a free tier of a few minutes, then a monthly plan. That hurts when you have a 1-hour podcast or 20 Shorts for the week. A speech model running on your own PC has no minute quota. This guide shows the free offline ways to caption long videos and whole folders, and how to fix the problems that only show up at that size.

First decide: caption track or burned-in?

  • Long video (talk, podcast, tutorial, vlog): make an SRT file and upload it to YouTube as a subtitle track. Viewers can switch it off, and you do not re-encode an hour of video.
  • Short clips (Shorts, Reels, TikTok): burn animated captions into the MP4. Many people watch with the sound off, and an uploaded subtitle file does not keep any styling.
  • Long video cut into clips: transcribe the long video once, then caption each finished clip. A clip has a different timeline than the source, so subtitles from the long video will not line up.

Way 1: Whisper on the command line (free, any length)

OpenAI's open-source Whisper runs on your PC (Python plus FFmpeg). One video:

whisper talk.mp4 --model small --language en --output_format srt

A whole folder on Windows (Command Prompt, run inside the folder):

for %f in (*.mp4) do whisper "%f" --model small --language en --output_format srt
  • Model: small is a good balance on a normal CPU. medium and large are more accurate, but much slower without an NVIDIA graphics card.
  • Language: set --language. Detection only listens to the first 30 seconds, and a long video with a music intro can be detected wrong.
  • Word timing: add --word_timestamps True if you want word-by-word captions later.
  • Faster: Faster-Whisper based tools (for example Purfview's Faster-Whisper-XXL) run the same models with less time and memory and skip silence (voice activity detection).

Way 2: Subtitle Edit batch mode (free, with a window)

If you prefer clicking to typing: in Subtitle Edit 5.4 open Video → Audio to text, switch from Single to Batch, add your files and start the run. The window shows the file list and a log. Then Video → Generate video with burned-in subtitles… also has a batch list, so it can burn in several videos one after another. For word-by-word and karaoke styles see Subtitle Edit word-by-word and karaoke captions.

Burn in many files with FFmpeg

With each SRT next to its video and with the same name (Command Prompt, inside the folder):

for %f in (*.mp4) do ffmpeg -i "%f" -vf "subtitles=%~nf.srt" -c:a copy "captioned_%~nf.mp4"

This gives plain subtitles. For styles and the tricky path quoting on Windows, see burn subtitles into a video for free.

Problems that only show up with long videos

The same line repeats again and again

  • In long pauses or music-only parts Whisper can get stuck on its previous sentence. Add --condition_on_previous_text False, or use a Faster-Whisper tool with voice activity detection.
  • Check the intro, outro and music breaks: text in those parts is often invented.

It takes hours

  • Time grows roughly with the length of the audio. Use a smaller model, an NVIDIA GPU, or a Faster-Whisper tool.
  • Test the settings on a 2-minute piece first, then start the long run overnight.

Captions slowly drift away from the voice

  • The subtitle belongs to a different version of the video (re-cut, intro added). Transcribe the final export.
  • Variable frame rate phone footage can confuse some editors. Convert it to a constant frame rate before editing.

Lines are far too long for a vertical clip

Sentence-level subtitles are made for wide video. For Shorts, split them into short lines or use word-level timing, see how to make word-by-word captions.

FAQ

How do I caption a long video for free and offline?

Run Whisper on your PC (command line or Subtitle Edit's Audio to text). No length limit. Upload the SRT to YouTube or burn it in with FFmpeg.

Can I caption many videos at once?

Yes: a for loop on the command line, or the Batch switch in Subtitle Edit 5.4's Audio to text.

Why does Whisper repeat the same line?

It gets stuck in long silent or music parts. Use --condition_on_previous_text False or a tool with voice activity detection.

Should I burn captions into a 1-hour video?

Usually not. Upload the SRT as a caption track. Burned-in animated captions are for short vertical clips.
