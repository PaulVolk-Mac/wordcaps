CapCut auto captions without Pro – 3 ways that still work

In 2025 CapCut moved auto captions into its paid Pro plan. If you open the captions panel you now see a Pro prompt. The bouncing one-word-at-a-time captions are still the standard look on TikTok, Shorts and Reels, so here are three ways to get them without a CapCut subscription – from free but technical to one click.

1. Free, technical: Whisper + FFmpeg

OpenAI's open-source speech model Whisper can output a timestamp for every word. Run it from the command line (for example with faster-whisper and word_timestamps=True), convert the words into an ASS subtitle file with one event per word, then burn it in with FFmpeg (-vf ass=captions.ass).

Cost: 0 €. Time: an evening of setup the first time, Python knowledge helps.

2. Free, manual styling: Subtitle Edit or Kdenlive

Subtitle Edit (Windows) and Kdenlive (Windows/Mac/Linux) can auto-transcribe with Whisper. You get an accurate SRT file. The karaoke-style highlight of the active word needs manual styling for each video. Import the SRT into CapCut for free and style it there as normal text.

3. One click, offline: WordCaps Studio (Windows, €4.99 once)

WordCaps Studio does option 1 for you, with a simple window instead of a command line:

  1. Export your edit from CapCut without captions.
  2. Drop the video into WordCaps Studio, choose one of 8 looks (classic, pop, box, minimal, bounce, karaoke, single word, neon), a colour and a size.
  3. Check the recognised text, fix names, press Burn.
  4. Upload the captioned MP4 – or import WordCaps Studio' SRT back into CapCut.

Everything runs on your PC: no account, no upload, no watermark, no subscription. Several clips can be captioned in one batch.

FAQ

Are CapCut auto captions still free?

Since CapCut's 2025 pricing change, auto captions show a Pro prompt for most users. Limits differ by region and app version – check inside your own CapCut app.

Can I import subtitles into CapCut for free?

Yes, importing an SRT file and styling it as text is possible without auto captions. That is why tools that export SRT are a good companion.

What is the most accurate free speech model?

OpenAI Whisper (open source, MIT licence). Larger models are more accurate but slower; "small" is a good balance on a normal PC.
