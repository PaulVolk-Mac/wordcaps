WordCaps Studio vs CapCut auto captions

Short answer: If you only need word-by-word captions on Windows and don't want a subscription, WordCaps Studio does that offline for a one-time €4.99. If you need a full editor with effects, music, templates and a phone app, CapCut is the better tool – but auto captions are part of its paid Pro plan since 2025.

Use both

Many creators edit in CapCut but don't want to pay Pro only for captions. Caption the finished clip with WordCaps Studio – or import WordCaps Studio' SRT file into CapCut and keep styling there.

When CapCut is the better choice

  • You edit on your phone or a Mac.
  • You already pay for Pro for other features.
  • You want trending templates, stickers and effects in the same tool.

CapCut's plans and features change often and differ by country. Check capcut.com for the current state. CapCut is a trademark of its owner; WordCaps Studio is independent and not affiliated.
