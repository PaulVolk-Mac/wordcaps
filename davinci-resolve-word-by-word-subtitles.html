Word-by-word subtitles in DaVinci Resolve (free version) – 3 ways

DaVinci Resolve is the best free video editor on Windows, but its automatic subtitles (Create Subtitles from Audio) only come with the paid DaVinci Resolve Studio. If you want TikTok- or Shorts-style word-by-word captions in the free version, these three ways work.

1. By hand on a subtitle track (free, slow)

Add a subtitle track (right-click the track header → Add Subtitle Track), then add one caption per word or per 2–3 words and move each one to the right spot. It works and costs nothing, but a one-minute clip has around 150 words. Fine for a short hook, not for every video.

Cost: 0 €. Time: 20–40 minutes per minute of talking.

2. Free, technical: Whisper SRT + import

The open-source speech model Whisper can write a timestamp for every word (for example faster-whisper with word_timestamps=True). Write one SRT entry per word or per short group, then import it in Resolve with File → Import → Subtitle and drag it onto the timeline. Style it in the Inspector (font, size, outline, position).

Limits: Resolve shows every entry in the same style – no highlighted active word, no pop-in animation, unless you build it yourself with Text+ and Fusion.

Cost: 0 €. Time: an evening of setup the first time, Python knowledge helps.

3. Offline in one window: WordCaps Studio (Windows, €4.99 once)

WordCaps Studio does the transcription on your PC and gives you two options:

  1. Burned-in animated captions: drop in your edit, pick one of 8 looks (classic, pop, box, minimal, bounce, karaoke, single word, neon), any colour and font, check the text, press Burn. The active word is highlighted, keywords can pop in a second colour.
  2. SRT/ASS for Resolve: the same run also saves an SRT with 1–5 words per entry. Import it in DaVinci Resolve (File → Import → Subtitle) if you want to keep the captions editable in your timeline.

No account, no upload, no watermark, no subscription. It is a captions tool, not a video editor – keep editing in Resolve.

FAQ

Can the free DaVinci Resolve make subtitles automatically?

No, automatic transcription is a Studio feature. The free version imports SRT files and lets you type subtitles by hand.

How many words per subtitle for vertical videos?

1–3 words read fastest on a phone. Keep them around the middle of the 9:16 frame, clear of the app buttons at the bottom and right edge.

Does WordCaps Studio work on Mac?

Not yet – Windows 10 and 11 only. On Mac, option 2 (Whisper + SRT) works.
