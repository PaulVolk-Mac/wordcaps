Free & offline Submagic alternatives for word-by-word captions

Submagic makes the animated one-word-at-a-time captions you see on TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Reels. It works in the browser, you upload your video, and the paid plans are a monthly subscription. If you only need the captions – or you don't want to upload your clips to a cloud – these four options do the same core job.

1. Whisper + FFmpeg (free, open source)

OpenAI's open-source speech model Whisper (or the faster faster-whisper) returns a timestamp for every word. Turn those words into an ASS subtitle file with one event per word, give the active word a different colour or size, and burn it in with ffmpeg -vf ass=captions.ass. You get exactly the Submagic look – after an evening of scripting.

Good for: developers and tinkerers. Downside: no window, no preview, every new style is code.

2. Subtitle Edit (free, Windows)

Subtitle Edit can auto-transcribe with Whisper and produces very accurate SRT files. It's the best free tool for correct subtitles. It does not animate words one by one, so you still need an editor for the short-form look.

3. CapCut (free editor, auto captions in Pro)

CapCut is a full video editor. Since its 2025 pricing change most users see a Pro prompt for auto captions. Importing an SRT file and styling it yourself is still possible on the free plan. More in our guide CapCut auto captions without Pro.

4. WordCaps Studio (Windows, €4.99 once, offline)

WordCaps Studio wraps option 1 in a simple window, so you get the Submagic-style result without the command line or a subscription:

FAQ

Is there a free alternative to Submagic?

Yes: Whisper + FFmpeg (free, open source, technical) and Subtitle Edit (free, accurate SRT, no animation). WordCaps Studio costs €4.99 once and gives the animated look without setup.

Is there an offline Submagic alternative?

WordCaps Studio runs 100 % offline on Windows – the video never leaves your PC. Whisper and Subtitle Edit also work offline.

Is there an open-source Submagic alternative?

The building blocks are open source (Whisper for word timestamps, FFmpeg + ASS for the burn-in). There is no single open-source app with the same polish yet.

Do the captions work in other languages?

Yes. WordCaps Studio detects the language automatically and supports English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Turkish and more.
