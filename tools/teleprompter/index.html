Keys, pauses and how the time is calculated

While it runs: Space or tap = pause / play · ↑ ↓ = faster / slower · ← → = jump back / ahead · + − = text size · M = mirror · Esc = close.

Pauses: write [pause] where you want to breathe – it adds 1 second and shows up dimmed. Each empty line adds half a second.

Time = words ÷ words per minute + pauses. Most people speak 130–160 words per minute on camera; fast TikTok talk is 170–200. Record one take and adjust the slider until the time matches.

Your script stays in this browser (local storage) and is never uploaded. The settings link only contains speed, size and mirror – not your text.
