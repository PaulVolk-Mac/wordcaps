White, pink or brown noise – what is the difference?

White noise has equal energy in every frequency – bright, like a TV between channels. Pink noise is softer in the highs, like steady rain. Brown noise is deep and rumbly, like a distant waterfall or a plane cabin – many people like it best for sleep and focus.

Rain, fan and ocean are built from the same noise with filters and slow movement. Everything is generated live in your browser – no recordings, no downloads, no tracking.

Tips: keep the volume low – it should mask sounds, not cover them. The sleep timer fades out over the last minute. On phones keep this tab open; some phones pause audio when the screen locks.
