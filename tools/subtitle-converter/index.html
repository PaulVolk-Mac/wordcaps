FAQ

How do I convert SRT to VTT?

Load or paste the .srt file, choose “VTT”, press Convert, then Download. Commas in timestamps become dots and the WEBVTT header is added.

How do I convert VTT to SRT?

Same steps with “SRT”. Cue settings (position, align) and styling tags are removed, cues are numbered.

Is my file uploaded?

No. The conversion is plain JavaScript in this page. You can even use it offline after it has loaded.

My subtitles are out of sync – can I fix that?

Yes. Enter a positive number to delay all subtitles or a negative number to show them earlier (e.g. -1.5).

Which formats are supported?

Input: SRT, VTT, ASS/SSA. Output: SRT, VTT, ASS, text only, text with timestamps.
