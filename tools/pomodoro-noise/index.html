How the Pomodoro technique works

Work on one task for one focus block (classic: 25 minutes), then take a short break (5 minutes). After four blocks take a long break (15–30 minutes). Longer blocks like 50/10 or 90/20 suit deep work.

Why noise? Brown noise and rain mask voices, typing and traffic, so there is less to react to. Keep it quiet – it should cover sounds, not drown them. Everything is generated live in your browser: no recordings, no tracking, works offline once loaded.

Tip: the remaining time shows in the browser tab, so you can switch tabs. On phones keep this tab open; some phones pause audio when the screen locks.
