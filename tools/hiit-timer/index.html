A free interval timer that just works

Set your work time, rest time and number of rounds, press Start and put your phone down. Three short beeps count you into every interval, a long beep starts it. The screen glows red for work, green for rest and yellow while you get ready, so you can read it from across the gym.

Presets: Tabata, HIIT 45/15, EMOM, Boxing

Tabata 20 s work / 10 s rest × 8 · HIIT 45/15 × 10 · HIIT 40/20 × 8 · 30/30 × 10 · EMOM 10 (every minute on the minute for 10 minutes) · Boxing 3 min rounds / 1 min rest × 5. Change any number and the link in your address bar updates, so you can bookmark your own workout.

Good to know

No ads, no account, nothing to install. Your settings stay in your browser. The screen stays awake while the timer runs (on browsers that support it), and the timer keeps exact time even if your phone is slow. Keyboard: Space = start/pause, → = skip, R = reset. Tip: add it to your home screen.
