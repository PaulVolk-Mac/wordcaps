How it is calculated

Protein in the pack = pack size × protein per 100 g ÷ 100.
Cost per 100 g protein = price ÷ protein in the pack × 100 (lower is better).
Protein per €/$ = protein in the pack ÷ price (higher is better).
kcal per g protein = kcal per 100 g ÷ protein per 100 g – handy when you cut: lower means leaner protein.

Label says “per bar” or “per serving”? Protein per 100 g = protein per bar ÷ bar weight × 100 (a 60 g bar with 20 g protein = 33 per 100 g). For dry foods (lentils, oats, rice) use the dry values from the pack.

The examples use rough supermarket prices to show how it works – type in the prices from your own store. This tool compares the amount of protein for the money, not taste or quality. It gives no nutrition or health advice.
