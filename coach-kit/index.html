What it takes off your plate

  • A plan per client

    Up to 60 rows per plan: day, exercise, sets, reps, rest, percent of 1RM and a note. Start empty or from one of two starting templates (2 and 4 days), and copy a finished plan to the next client.

  • Target kg without a calculator

    Enter a percentage. The app takes this client's best estimated 1RM from the log and shows the load, rounded to 2.5 kg. No log entry yet? The field stays empty, the app does not guess.

  • Who needs a message?

    The Today view lists every client with 14 days or more without a log entry, with a ready message you can copy and send.

  • One log, a progress sheet per client

    Date, client, exercise, weight, reps. The Progress sheet shows the first, best and latest estimated 1RM per exercise with the change and a trend line, ready to print or save as a PDF.

  • Weeks 1–4 in the plan

    Switch on a weekly progression per plan: +2.5 % weight per week, or +1 rep per week. The plan and the PDF then show weeks 1 to 4 per exercise. Weights need a % 1RM and a logged set; reps only go up for plain numbers like “5” or “6–10”.

  • Ready to send as a PDF

    Print or save the plan as a PDF: a clean sheet, grouped by day, with your name or brand on top and your note to the client.

  • Your data stays yours

    No account, no cloud. The app itself makes no network request at all.

How it works

  1. Open it: unzip, double-click CoachKit.html. Type your name or brand and add your clients. “Load sample data” lets you look around first.
  2. Plan: pick the client, add exercises, enter sets, reps, rest and a percentage. Copy the plan to another client if it fits.
  3. Log: enter the client's sets. The estimated 1RM, the target kg in the plan and the client's status update by themselves.
  4. Send it: print or save the plan as a PDF and send it to your client.

Where your data lives

Please read before you buy. Everything is stored in the browser you open the file with, on that device. There is no sync and no server. Another browser or device starts empty, and clearing the browser data deletes your clients, plans and log. The app has a “Save backup file” button and reminds you when changes have been in no backup file for 7 days. Loading that file brings everything back, also on another device.

Questions

Where is my client data stored?

Only in the browser you open CoachKit.html with, on that device. Nothing is sent anywhere and there is no account. That also means: another browser or device starts empty, and clearing the browser data deletes it. So save a backup file regularly. The app reminds you when changes have been in no backup file for 7 days.

Can I use it on my phone or a second computer?

The layout works at phone width. There is no sync, though. To move your data, save a backup file on one device and load it on the other.

Which browsers does it work in?

It was tested in Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome on Windows, opened as a local file, at desktop and phone width. Firefox and Safari (including iPhone) were not tested. If something breaks there, write to me. Best used on one device, with backups saved.

How exact is the estimated 1RM?

It is an estimate from the Epley rule of thumb, not a tested maximum. The app only calculates for 1 to 10 reps, because such formulas are reported to get less accurate above about 10 reps. The sources are listed in the README.

Can I add my logo?

No. Your name or brand appears as the headline on every plan you print or save as a PDF. A logo upload is not included.

Are training plans included?

No. This is a tool for coaches to build their own plans. It suggests exercise names in English and German, it comes with two starting templates (2 and 4 days) that you change line by line, not a program library, and it is not coaching or medical advice.

Can I use it with paying clients?

Yes. You may use it commercially with your own clients and send them the plans. You may not resell or share the kit itself.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and download a ZIP. The app is a single file on your own computer and needs no internet.
