Questions

Does it work in Google Sheets?

It was built and tested in Microsoft Excel for Windows. A static check found no formula that Google Sheets lacks, but it has not been test-imported there yet. If something breaks there, write to me.

How exact is the estimated 1RM?

It is an estimate from two common rules of thumb (Epley and Brzycki), not a tested maximum. The kit only calculates for 1 to 10 reps, because such formulas are reported to get less accurate above about 10 reps. The sources are listed in the README.

Does it place my logo automatically?

No. You insert your logo once per sheet with Insert > Pictures. A spreadsheet without macros cannot do that by itself. Your name or brand does appear on every plan automatically.

Are training plans included?

No. This is a tool for coaches to build their own plans. It contains an exercise list, not ready-made programs, and it is not coaching or medical advice.

Can I use it with paying clients?

Yes. You may use it commercially with your own clients and send them the plans. You may not resell or share the kit itself.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and download a ZIP with two Excel files. Your client data stays in your own file on your own computer.
