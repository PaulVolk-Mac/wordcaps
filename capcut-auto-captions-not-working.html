CapCut auto captions not working? 8 fixes

You press Captions → Auto captions and nothing happens: the button is greyed out, the progress bar hangs, or CapCut finishes with no text or with nonsense in the wrong language. Most of these cases have a simple cause. Go through the checks in this order – the first ones solve the most cases.

1. A Pro badge or a greyed-out button: the feature is locked

Since CapCut's 2025 pricing change, auto captions sit behind CapCut Pro for most users. If you see a Pro badge, a crown or an upgrade prompt, your video is fine – the feature is simply not available on your plan. What is free differs by region, platform (desktop, mobile, web) and app version, so check inside your own app. If you don't want a subscription, jump to the workarounds below.

2. "No speech recognised" or empty captions: the voice is too quiet

Recognition listens to the mixed audio of the selected clip. Loud background music, wind or a quiet voice make it give up. Fixes:

  • Mute or lower the music track while you generate captions, then turn it back up.
  • Raise the volume of the voice clip, or use CapCut's noise reduction on it first.
  • Check the clip is not muted and its audio was not separated onto a track you muted.

3. Captions in the wrong language or full of nonsense

In the auto captions panel you choose the spoken language. If it is set to English but you speak German (or the other way round), the result is gibberish or empty. Select the language that is actually spoken in the clip. Mixed-language videos work best when you caption each part separately.

4. Stuck at 0 % or "network error": check your connection

CapCut's speech recognition runs on CapCut's servers, not on your device. A weak Wi-Fi, a VPN, a firewall or a company network can make it hang or fail. Switch networks, turn the VPN off for a moment and try again. Very long projects also take longer to upload – try a shorter test clip first.

5. Only some clips get captions: the wrong clips are selected

Depending on the version, CapCut captions either the whole timeline or only the selected clips. Select all clips with speech (or none, if your version captions everything) and run it again. Voice-overs on their own audio track are sometimes skipped – make sure the voice track is included.

6. Old captions get mixed with new ones

If you run auto captions twice, you can end up with duplicated or overlapping text. Turn on the option to clear current captions before generating new ones, or delete the old caption track by hand.

7. Update the app and clear the cache

Old CapCut versions and a full cache cause odd failures. Update CapCut, restart it, and on desktop clear the cache in the settings. On a phone, also check that there is enough free storage.

8. Try another CapCut platform

Desktop, mobile and the web editor do not always have the same features unlocked. If captions fail on one, open the same project on another platform where you are logged in with the same account – sometimes it works there.

FAQ

Why are my CapCut auto captions greyed out?

In most regions auto captions are part of CapCut Pro since 2025. A Pro badge or a greyed-out button usually means the feature is locked for your account, not that your video is broken.

Why does CapCut say no speech was recognised?

The voice is too quiet compared to music, the clip is muted or its audio was separated, or the wrong spoken language is selected.

Do CapCut auto captions work offline?

No, recognition runs on CapCut's servers and needs an internet connection. Whisper-based tools run fully on your own PC.

Can I import my own subtitles into CapCut?

Yes. Importing an SRT file and styling it as text works without auto captions.
