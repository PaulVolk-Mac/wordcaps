Word-by-word captions in Premiere Pro – one word at a time, 3 ways

Short-form videos read best with one to three words on screen. Premiere Pro can do this now, but how depends on your version – and the TikTok look with a highlighted active word is still not built in. Here are the three ways that work.

1. Premiere 26.3 or newer: the Single Word option

Adobe added single-word captions in the June 2026 update (Premiere 26.3). Open Window → Text, transcribe the sequence on the Transcript tab, click Create captions and pick Single Word. Premiere syncs every word to the audio. Then style the caption track (font, size, stroke) and place it in the middle of the 9:16 frame, clear of the app buttons.

Cost: included in the Premiere Pro subscription. Limit: one style for all words – no colour on the spoken word, no pop-in.

2. Older versions: the “7 characters” workaround

Before 26.3 there was no single-word switch. The common workaround from the Adobe forums: in Create captions set Lines: Single, Maximum length in characters to the lowest value the dialog allows (often 7), and Minimum duration to 0. Most captions then hold one word; long words and short pairs like “I am” still end up together, so expect a few minutes of manual splitting per clip. Users report that the minimum duration can override the character limit – set it to 0 first.

Cost: subscription. Time: a few minutes of clean-up per minute of talking.

3. Highlighted active word, offline: WordCaps Studio (Windows, €4.99 once)

If you want the look that Submagic, Captions.ai or CapCut Pro sell – the spoken word lights up, keywords pop in a second colour – without a plugin or subscription:

  1. Export your edit from Premiere (or use the raw clip).
  2. Drop it into WordCaps Studio, pick one of 8 looks (classic, pop, box, minimal, bounce, karaoke, single word, neon), any colour and font. It transcribes on your PC.
  3. Check the text, press Burn. You get an MP4 with animated word-by-word captions, plus an SRT with 1–5 words per entry.
  4. Back in Premiere: put the burned MP4 on the timeline, or import the SRT (File → Import) if you want editable captions.

No account, no upload, no watermark, no subscription. It is a captions tool, not an editor – keep cutting in Premiere.

FAQ

Where is the Single Word option in Premiere Pro?

In the Create captions dialog (Text panel → Transcript → Create captions), from Premiere 26.3 on. If you don’t see it, update Premiere or use the workaround in way 2.

Can Premiere Pro make karaoke captions?

Not automatically. You can fake it with two stacked caption tracks and keyframes, which is slow. WordCaps Studio has a karaoke look that fills each word as it is spoken.

How many words per caption for Reels, Shorts and TikTok?

1–3 words read fastest on a phone. Use a bold font with a dark outline, centre it slightly below the middle of the frame.

Does WordCaps Studio work on Mac?

Not yet – Windows 10 and 11 only. On Mac, use the Premiere options above.
